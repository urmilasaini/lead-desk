How Lead Desk works

From a raw inquiry to "call this person now", in a few seconds.

The flow

Three steps for every lead

1Add

Add a lead with the form, or import many at once from a CSV file. Paste the customer's message in English, Hinglish or as a WhatsApp chat.

2Analyze

The AI reads the message and pulls out the facts. A fixed scoring rubric turns them into a 0–100 score, then the AI writes your next step and a reply.

3Act

Call the hottest leads first, send the reply on WhatsApp, ask the AI questions about the lead, and log each call so the score stays current.

How the score works

The AI finds the facts. The score itself comes from fixed rules, so the same lead always gets the same score and every point has a reason.

FactorPointsWhat it looks at
Budget fit20Is the budget realistic for that area and property type?
Funds ready15Loan sanctioned, pre-approved or paying from own funds
Timeline20How soon they want to buy
Clear need15Property type, area and a real reason, like a job move, lease ending or school admission
Buying intent15Asked for a visit or call, already visited, ready to book, asked specific questions
Decision maker10Decides alone, or needs someone else's approval
Detail given5How many concrete details the message contains
Hot · 70+

Call today. Strong fit and real urgency.

Warm · 40–69

Interested, but something is missing. Follow up within days.

Cold · under 40

Just browsing. Send a light follow-up.

  • Not mentioned doesn't mean no. If a customer doesn't mention a loan, that factor gets a neutral score, not zero.
  • Objections don't lower the score. A customer who says "match the other builder's price and I'll book" is serious. Objections appear with ways to handle them.
  • Big deals get a call. Leads of ₹3 Cr or more never drop to Cold.
  • Spam and wrong cities are Disqualified. They don't take up your time.
  • Scores change over time. If the customer goes quiet for 3, 7 or 14 days the score drops. A site visit or negotiation raises it.
  • You have the final say. Use “Change priority” on a lead and give a reason. It's marked as changed, and the original AI priority stays visible.

Your day with Lead Desk

Before the call

Open Today's calls for the best order to call. A red ⏱ means a lead has been waiting too long.

During the call

Use the talking points on the lead page, or ask the AI chat: “What should I emphasize?”

After the call

Tap Log call, add rough notes, and the score updates. You also get a follow-up message to send.

Priority vs. pipeline stage

Priority (Hot / Warm / Cold) says how likely and how urgent a lead is. The AI sets it, and it can change as new information arrives.

Stage says where the deal is: New → Contacted → Site visit → Negotiation → Booked (or Lost). You move it, either by dragging cards on the Pipeline page or from the lead page. Logging a call moves the stage forward for you.

Importing many leads

  1. Go to Leads → Import CSV.
  2. Use a file with the columns name, phone, location, requirement, budget, timeline, message. Only name and message are required, and up to 50 leads per file.
  3. Each lead is analysed one by one. If one doesn't finish, it is still saved. Use Re-analyze on it, or Open it.
